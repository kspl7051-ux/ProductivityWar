Effective Date: May 18, 2026

KSquare System Private Limited ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Productivity War mobile application ("App"). This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, and protected when using our application.

By using Productivity War, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. About Productivity War

Productivity War is a productivity and focus application designed to help users improve concentration and work habits through timer-based sessions, activities, reports, and statistics.

Current features include:

  • Productivity timer sessions
  • Activity tracking
  • Statistics
  • Reports

The application currently operates primarily with locally stored data on the user's device.

2. Information We Collect

At this time, Productivity War does not require user account creation and generally does not collect personal information directly.

Current data may include:

  • Productivity session information
  • Activity history
  • Statistics and reports generated inside the application
  • App settings and preferences

This information is stored locally on your device.

We currently do not intentionally collect:

  • Contact information
  • Photos or videos
  • Payment information
  • Advertising identifiers
  • Precise location data

Productivity War currently uses Expo framework and related services.
